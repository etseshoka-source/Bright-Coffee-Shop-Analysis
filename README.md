# Bright-Coffee-Shop-Analysis
This project analyzes Bright Coffee Shop historical sales data to discover insights into sales performance, customer behavior and product trends. 

##Aim
To provide the new CEO with an analysis that will assist in making an informed decision to enhance revenue and operational effeciency.

##Objectives
-To analyze sales distribution by product category
-Calculate the total revenue and analyze sales perfomance
-Discover the top selling products and the best performing store overall
-Provide actionable insights recommendations

####Tools used
-Miro for planning
-Canva for project planning
-SQL(Databricks) for quering and aggregating data
-Excel for creating pivot charts and generate insights
-Canva for presentation

###Summary
The analysis shows that the hot beverages especially coffee and tea are the core drivers of revenue. Therefore, focusing on the top-performing products and improving underperforming categories, the business can significantly enhance profitability.
